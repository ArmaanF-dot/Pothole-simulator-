# 150-word summary (submission form)

Southampton fills about 4,000 potholes a year, and in four of the last five years spent nothing on preventative
maintenance. A reactive repair costs £87.80, a planned one £56.94.

RoadSense helps Southampton's highways officers decide which roads to treat this week, before potholes form. A model trained on real FixMyStreet reports from three south-coast cities scores every council
road using traffic counts, bus routes, flood zones, freeze-thaw forecasts and pothole history. It explains each
score, ranks a weekly plan by repair cost avoided per pound, and triages residents' reports into work orders an
officer approves. On unseen reports, its top tenth of roads caught 42% of next year's reported potholes in Southampton and 31%
in Brighton, against 34% and 22% for last year's reports.

Councils or their contractors pay an annual licence. Every input is national open data, so another city needs only its
boundary and repair records.
